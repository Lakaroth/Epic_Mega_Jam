# BLOOD & SAND — 2026 Epic AI MegaJam

A short, cinematic spaghetti-western game made in Unreal Engine 5.8.4 for the
[2026 Epic AI MegaJam](https://itch.io/jam/2026-epic-ai-megajam) (theme: **DIRECTOR'S CUT**). Working title: *Director's Cut*.

- **Play it:** [elvismorelli.itch.io/blood-sand](https://elvismorelli.itch.io/blood-sand) (Windows 10/11, 64-bit)
- **Jam entry:** [itch.io/jam/2026-epic-ai-megajam/rate/5377370](https://itch.io/jam/2026-epic-ai-megajam/rate/5377370)

You play the gunman, and you direct the film: wherever there are cameras, the D-pad cuts live between fixed
cinematic shots and your own third-person camera, while you keep full control of the gameplay.

## What this repository is

This is the **public development journal and documentation** for the project. It is
**not** a mirror of the Unreal project. The game's source assets, purchased content,
MetaHuman source files, binaries and vendor packages stay private and local.
