# Third-party content — submission declaration

**Game:** BLOOD & SAND (working title: Director's Cut), 2026 Epic AI MegaJam, by Elvis Morelli (Lakaroth).
**Status as of:** 2026-11-16, final. The Status column was checked against the file list of the shipped Windows package (version 2 on itch.io); see "Final build check" in [ASSET_PROVENANCE.md](ASSET_PROVENANCE.md). Rows for content that does not ship are kept and marked "Not in the build". The "Use in the game" texts were written during the jam: where they say "Planned", the Status column gives the final answer.

None of the content below was made by the entrant. Each item is used under the license from its source and ships only inside the compiled game. No source files are redistributed: the public repository holds text documentation only.

Full records, with evidence and open questions, are in [ASSET_PROVENANCE.md](ASSET_PROVENANCE.md).
